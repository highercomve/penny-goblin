# Friendly Mischief

Penny Goblin makes personal bookkeeping approachable through a cheerful collector of payment slips. The mascot combines a broad pointed-ear silhouette with a small receipt, so the task is visible before any words are read. Its expression is alert and mischievous, never threatening.

Forest outlines, bright leaf green, warm ivory and a coral confirmation mark form a deliberately small palette. Careful contrast gives the character a clear edge on both light paper and dark application surfaces. The receipt remains the quiet visual anchor beneath the face.

Meticulously spaced eyes, a small fang and an asymmetric smile provide personality without a detailed fantasy scene. The silhouette carries the identity at small scales; larger uses reveal the hands, paper edge and checkmark. The craft comes from restraint and painstaking attention to visual balance.

The primary mark contains no lettering. The application pairs it with a separate Penny Goblin wordmark, preserving legibility and accessibility. The transparent original lives in frontend/logo.png; icon.png is its 1024-pixel application derivative. The original mascot was generated specifically for this application with the image-generation tool. The application identity is com.pennygoblin.app across platforms; desktop data and pairing identity are preserved during the namespace migration.

## Minimal line revision

The current mark uses a rounded monoline silhouette, dot eyes and a gentle smile. Hands, eyebrows, fang and paper lines were removed for a friendlier, modern small-scale identity. The first mascot remains in logo-v1.png as a reference.

## Linux tray mark

The tray uses a dedicated filled goblin face in leaf green and forest rather
than shrinking the detailed application mark. Broad ears, two dark eyes and
a small smile remain legible at 16–24 px. `tray.svg` is the vector source;
`src/tray.png` is its 64 px transparent raster for Oriel's portable tray API.
Regenerate with `rsvg-convert -w 64 -h 64 brand/tray.svg -o src/tray.png`.
The application, Android and in-app logos retain the full receipt mark.
